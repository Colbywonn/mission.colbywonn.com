Spiritual thing:
Literally just Joseph Smith's 1838 testimony:
I had actually seen a light, and in the midst of that light I saw two
Personages, and they did in reality speak to me; and though I was hated and
persecuted for saying that I had seen a vision, yet it was true; and while
they were persecuting me, reviling me, and speaking all manner of evil
against me falsely for so saying, I was led to say in my heart: Why
persecute me for telling the truth? I have actually seen a vision; and who
am I that I can withstand God, or why does the world think to make me deny
what I have actually seen? For I had seen a vision; I knew it, and I knew
that God knew it, and I could not deny it, neither dared I do it; at least
I knew that by so doing I would offend God, and come under condemnation.
Gotta be one of the hardest lines in scripture. "I knew it, and I knew God
knew it"
