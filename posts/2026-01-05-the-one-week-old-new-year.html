The One Week Old New Year

Hey Guys!!!
So so so sorry, I'm getting more and more inconsistent. I sometimes get
lazy with them. I started writing this a wee bit behind schedule so it'll
be a bit short.
Quick scripture:
In my persoal studies, I've been trying to study the Old Testament. Well,
read it. Before I go home. I'm in Joshua right now. Its a lot more fun to
read than the 5 books of Moses 💀
Except for Genesis. That book is crazy.
But anyway its pretty fun to read in Deuteronomy and randomly stumble upon
a baller Jesus quote:
Chapter 8 btw
3 And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with
manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might
make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that
proceedeth out of the mouth of the Lord doth man live.

Bars, Moses, BARS.
It wasn't the Mana that led Israel through the desert. It was God. He didnt
just sustain them physically, he led them and prepared them spiritually.
How does God do that in our lives? How do we choose to live not just by
bread, but by every word that He says to us? What is His word? Well,
there's the word of God and there's the Word of God. Both are needed. The
word of God is the scriptures, whether that be from a holy book or the word
of His living prophet. The Word, capital W, is Jesus Christ. (See John 1:1)
we need his words, and his Word in order to live.
John 6
53 Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except ye eat
the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you.

The Sacrement is yet another way we can live off of the Word that
proceedeth from God.

2 Nephi 32
3 ...feast upon the words of Christ; for behold, the words of Christ will
tell you all things what ye should do.
So much eating, so much feasting! The Isrealites would feast to remember
the salvation of the Lord, and we do, too. The Sacrement of the Lord's
Supper is a spiritual feast! When we prepare for it, we get to eat the
words of God, and the Word of God*.
John 6 (again)
55 For my flesh is meat indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.

But anyway for a quick breakdown of the last 2 weeks
Christmas!!! Very fun. We ate dinner at a member's house and then we went
to see Christmas lights.

Elder's Conference!!! Like Zone Conference but just for Elders.
We are still doing service at the Houston Food Bank which is really fun. We
are also still sharing a car which is really, really fun!

Heres a quick not-having-a-car miracle that happened tonight:
We were walking to our area, when we passed by a gas station. We hadn't
done daily contacts because it was pday so we decided to stop and go inside
and call up some folks. There was a woman filling up her car who waved us
over. She said she was a member, and that her mom was in a rest home just a
few hundred feet away. She asked us if we could go over and give her mom a
blessing. We did! It was so amazing that the Lord put us in her path that
night so she (and her mom) could be comforted. The timing of the Lord is
impeccable, for real.
