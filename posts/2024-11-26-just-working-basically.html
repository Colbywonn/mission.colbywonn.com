I'm gonna keep this one briefish because its kinda late! But anyway I just
wanna give an update about my spiritual thought last week that I discovered
while I was studying it, that Alma 5 is chiasmic. Ok so a brief
explanation, its a memory device the scriptures use a lot, that exists
extensively in Hebrew but not so much in English. Basically, you start with
idea A, move to idea B, repeat idea B, go back to idea A. So ABBA. A short
scriptual example would be "first is last, last is first". So, first (A),
last (B), last (B), first (A). This can go in as far as you want, so like
ABCDEFGHIJKJIHGFEDCBA. The best example of this in all of literature is
Alma 36. It goes in something like 17 layers deep (all the way to Q).
Insane! This also wasn't discovered until 1968!! The Book of Mormon is
true. (Also, I have been reading it on my mission cover to cover and I've
only made it to Mosiah.) (So in twice the time it took to translate, I've
read less than half. Wow.) Anyway, I figured out that Alma 5 was a chiasm
while I was looking at it in zone conference. Alma 5:14-26 follows this
pattern, and also I think basically the entire chapter as a whole. Idk I
just think it's neat.

Not the scriptural thought, this is it though:
It's from Alma 32,
18. Now I ask, is this faith? Behold, I say unto you, Nay; for if a man
knoweth a thing he hath no cause to believe, for he knoweth it.
I really like this scripture because well it does a great job of describing
what the word faith is, or rather what it isn't. I think that sometimes
that concept is overlooked a lot. Faith is not knowledge, it is hope.
Knowledge is extremely dangerous, especially to know of God for certain.
19 And now, how much more cursed is he that knoweth the will of God and
doeth it not, than he that only believeth, or only hath cause to believe,
and falleth into transgression?
To have a knowledge of God and to sin is to be in open, active, rebellion.
There are only two sins that are unforgivable, to shed innocent blood and
to blaspheme the Holy Ghost. To be in rebellion against the God, you must
first acknowledge that there is, in fact, a God. So, to blaspheme the Holy
Ghost you would first have to know God exists. Knowledge is power, and with
great power comes great responsibility. Faith protects us. We can fail, we
don't have a perfect knowledge. We can repent and become better because of
faith. How much of a blessing is that? You don't have a perfect knowledge,
you just need to belive. You need to have faith. That's why it's the first
principle of the gospel. It is completely fundamental for all other things.
You don't even need to have faith in everything. Just in the divinity of
Jesus Christ. So, I have a question for you. Do you belive you have a
personal savior? Do you have place in your heart to belive that? To hope
it? If not, it's time to plant a seed. Place just the smallest bit of faith
in the truth that Jesus Christ is your savior. Pray about it. Study the
scriptures. Your hope will grow. The gospel of Jesus Christ brings hope,
not knowledge. And I am eternally grateful for that.
