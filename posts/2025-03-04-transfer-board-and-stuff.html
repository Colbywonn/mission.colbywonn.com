Ok anyway heres the thought:
This is a quote from Elder Bruce R. McConkie, in a talk from April 1971:
And may I say that the only way to gain true religion is to receive it from
the Lord. True religion is revealed religion; it is not a creation of man’s
devising; it comes from God. Man did not create God, nor can he redeem
himself. No man can resurrect himself or assign himself to an inheritance
in a heavenly kingdom. Salvation comes from God, on his terms, and the
things men must do to gain it can be known only by revelation.
This is baller. Religion is about God, not man. This talk is all about
revelation. Elder McConkie talks about how revelation is like radio
signals. It's always around us, but if we don't notice it unless we tune
into it. How do we tune into it? Well, with a radio and antenna. Without a
radio, we don't have a way to take those incoming signals and turn them
into understandable sound. That radio is the Holy Ghost. When we get the
gift of the holy ghost, we replace our weak old lame radios that only work
half of the time to high-range always-on radios. Its still up to us to turn
the radio on and listen. Like, for example, General Conference! Thats kinda
low key already happeneing again, it feels like it was forever ago but it's
only like a month out. Maybe its about time we started prepping our radios
for that broadcast.
This was a good week. We worked hard, had fun, and got transfer news. I'm
going to stay in Galena Park. Elder Soucie is moving to Lake Charles :( its
the furthest area from me, and thered a good chamce he stays out there for
the rest of his mission. He only has 3 transfers left, so I'll never see
him again haha. It was a fun transfer, I sure love Elder Soucie! My new
comp is named Elder Sanchez, I don't think I've ever really talked to him
but he seems cool. Elder Condie, who served in my same district for my
first 3 transfers (the 6'9" one) will be living in my quad instead of Elder
Card, so he'll finish Elder Black's training! Very neat. Not gonna lie, I'm
not really sure what else happened this week besides a suprise zone meeting
with president. He told us 3 things that we needed to do as a mission to
make sure we were following the standards better and be safe. He told us
that he recived revelation that if we dont do as good at staying with our
comps at all times, something bad could happen. He also told us to make
sure our messages to other missionaries was purpose focused, and that we
shouldnt use our phone outside of view from our companions. Nothing new,
just a re-emphasis. That's kinda how learning with the Holy Ghost works! We
hear things that we've already heard and we learn that it's true through
its power.
