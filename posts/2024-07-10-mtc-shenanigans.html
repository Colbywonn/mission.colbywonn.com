LETTER 001  —  WEDNESDAY, 10 JULY 2024 · week 3

MTC Shenanigans

What's up, gamers!
It's my first PDAY at the MTC! I've been pretty busy, lemme tell you. I've
had two 3-hour classes a day! Either tomorrow or the day after, we'll be
switching to full Spanish in our classes. I definitely don't feel prepared
yet but I suppose as time goes on I'll slowly get better and better at
Spanish. The comps and the district are awesome! I love every single one of
em, and I'm so grateful the Lord chose us to be together. I have two
companions right now, Elder Saez and Elder Mauchley. Elder Saez's companion
couldn't start the MTC for whatever reason, so we were put into a trio.
There's a total of 5 Elders in our room, so my companions and one other
pair of Elders from my district.
[image: image.png]
My District, 26-H! There are 3 Hermanas and 7 Elders. From me (Elder
Miller), moving clockwise, we have Elder Corgatelli, Elder Olivas
(roommate), Elder Useche, Hermana Jones, Hermana Cox, Hermana Brown (the
one taking the picture), Elder Saez (my comp), Elder Mauchley (my other
comp), and Elder Pennock (roommate). We're sitting on top of one of the
buildings on the MTC. There's a super duper nice area I like to call "the
patio" where we go to study, with a grassy spot and some pretty flowers. I
didn't take any photos of the flowers, sorry :( .
The 4th of July also happened while I was here last
week 🦅🦅🦅🇺🇲🇺🇲🇺🇲. I'd been there for less than a day and so the
fireworks added more time onto an already long day. We got to watch the
Stadium of Fire fireworks from the main area in the MTC. There was a drone
show too, which was really cool to watch. Not much else happened that week,
besides lots and lots of learning and practice. Sunday was awesome, we
fasted for a lot of people together as a district and it was really cool to
be able to think of and pray for so many people at once. We also had what
was perhaps the best Elder's Quorum meeting ever. One of the councillors on
the branch presidency, Brother Grant, shared how he brings the spirit into
conversations and spreads the gospel, and it WORKS. The most important part
of converting people is the feeling of the Holy Ghost. If the Holy Ghost
isn't present, no one would ever feel the truth of the gospel. It is by His
power that we can convert people. The spirit is invited when truth is
shared. Moses 6:61 in the Pearl of Great Price says:

61. Therefore it is given to abide in you; the record of heaven; the
Comforter; the peaceable things of immortal glory; the truth of all things;
that which quickeneth all things, which maketh alive all things; that which
knoweth all things, and hath all power according to wisdom, mercy, truth,
justice, and judgment.

It's really crazy to think of how powerful the spirit really is! The power
of the Holy Ghost we feel when truths are said is the same power that
parted the Red Sea. Anyway, along with this scripture, Brother Grant also
shared the best way to start any conversation about the gospel: "In 1820, a
boy said a prayer" Let me tell you friends, the spirit RUSHES in when those
words are uttered. It's crazy how STRONG the spirit is when you share the
story of Joseph Smith's first vision in his exact words. My comps also
dragged me to choir practice, so I found out I was a tenor on Sunday. On
Tuesday, Elder Olivas had to fly to Phoenix and back to get his visa
cleared. He needed to visit the Ecuadorian consulate, and they require
missionaries to go in person to the consulate for their visas. He left at
like 3:00AM and got back at 10:30PM. That night, we had a general authority
talk given by President Mark Pace, the Sunday School General President. He
and his wife talked to us about the power of prayer, and of joy. I learned
a lot about prayer, and also reflected on just how much prayer invites the
spirit. Today, I got to visit the Mount Timpanogos Temple this morning.
Unfortunately, I am out of time to write how it went but it went well.
Thanks for reading my email, I haven't proofread it so be nice to me. I'll
talk to y'all next week! feel free to send me your favorite scriptures or
just reach out.
