Scripture time!
In my personal study right now, I'm in the firt part of the book of Mosiah.
This part of the Book of Mormon is the discourse of Kind Benjamin, where he
exorts his people to repent and also shares with them the conditions of
salvation: (Chapter 4)
8 And this is the means whereby salvation cometh. And there is none other
salvation save this which hath been spoken of; neither are there any
conditions whereby man can be saved except the conditions which I have told
you.
9 Believe in God; believe that he is, and that he created all things, both
in heaven and in earth; believe that he has all wisdom, and all power, both
in heaven and in earth; believe that man doth not comprehend all the things
which the Lord can comprehend.
10 And again, believe that ye must repent of your sins and forsake them,
and humble yourselves before God; and ask in sincerity of heart that he
would forgive you; and now, if you believe all these things see that ye do
them.
Wow! Look at that right there! Its the gispel of Jesus Christ again! I'm
gonna be so honest with you, I didnt know that this process was called the
Gospel of Jesus Christ before my mission. In case you don't know, the
Gospel of Jesus Christ are the things we need to do to attain salvation.
Nothing more and nothing less.
The first step is to have faith, IE: Belive in God.
The second step is to repent.
The third step is the initiative ordinance of baptism. Baptism is an
outward expression of your repentance. When you are baptized, you become a
covenant child of Jesus Christ.
The fourth step is baptism by fire and the Holy Ghost. This ordinance is
just as important as baptism by water. Joseph Smith said that a baptism by
water without the gift of the holy ghost avails nothing. Both are required,
and are individual ordinances.
The fifth Is to endure to the end. When we endure to the end we stay as
true to our promises as we can. When we promise that, the Lord promises us
blessings, such as peace, joy, the holy ghost, and most importantly,
salvation. Because of the sacrifice, we can all recive this gift. Not much
is required to be honest. We are so blessed to have such an amazing way to
be redeemed! Pretty cool!
