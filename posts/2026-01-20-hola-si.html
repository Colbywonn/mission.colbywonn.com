Ok, now I want to share something with you
This is from John. Or rather, Juan. Capítulo 14.
18. No os dejaré huérfanos; vendré a vosotros.
18. I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

The word I wanna focus on is rendered as "comfortless" in English and as
"huérfanos" in Spanish. In the Greek, the word is probably better
translated as "Orphaned" which is the word in Spanish. Yet another example
of how the Bible was translated incorrectly!!!!! But anyway, how much
cooler is the verse that way? "I will not leave you orphaned..."
When we are baptised, we are spiritually adopted by Jesus. He becomes the
Father of our covenant. That's why Jesus Christ is sometimes also referred
to as the Father. That, and he has the same power and authority as the
Father, plus he created the world. I find that idea helps enlighten me on
verses such as this:
Mosiah 16:15 Teach them that redemption cometh through Christ the Lord, who
is the very Eternal Father. Amen.
Or this:
Alma 11:38 Now Zeezrom saith again unto him: Is the Son of God the very
Eternal Father?
39 And Amulek said unto him: Yea, he is the very Eternal Father of heaven
and of earth, and all things which in them are; he is the beginning and the
end, the first and the last;
Sometimes its confusing, but thats ok! We arent asked to understand
everything, but we have been asked to "believe all things, [and] hope all
things"
Believe that Jesus Loves you, believe that he uses His church to bless your
life, and that is enough. The rest will follow, I promise.

Thats all for this week, folks!
