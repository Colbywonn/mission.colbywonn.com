LETTER 020  —  MONDAY, 9 DECEMBER 2024 · week 25

Orange Juice

Hello all,
How was your week? Good, I hope! It's almost Christmas, it's practically
here! This week has been pretty dope! A whole lotta working!
I guess in the most important news, this week was transfer week! Well, I
move on Wednesday, but we got the transfer information Saturday night. I
will be moving for the first time in my mission, to a town called Orange.
Orange is right on the border between Texas and Louisiana. Literally on the
other side of the mission. My area is the only Spanish area in the entire
district, and one of I think 3 in the entire zone. Not a whole lotta
Spanish speakers there haha! I'm pretty excited to be able to explore a new
area but I'm a little nervous.
My new companion's name is Elder Card. He was in the MTC at the same time
as me, just in Mexico City. He was actually Elder Condie's companion, the
guy who's 6'9" in all my photos! We're also whitewashing in, which means
both the missionaries that are already there are moving out and we're
moving in.
Park Place, the area I'm in now, is getting sisters moved into it. I think
it should hopefully be really good for the area! I'm definitely sad to
leave some of the people I'm teaching here, but the Lord has a plan!
Speaking of the Lord, spiritual thought time!
This week's thought brought by Moroni 8, one of my favorite chapters in all
of holy writ. The Bible really is tragically unclear on the topic of child
baptism (or, as Elder James E. Talmage would say, pedobaptism). Well,
fortunately for Latter-Day Saints, we have our answer! From the Book of
Mormon, we learn that child baptism is not only not needed but an
abomination. All children are saved by him whose grace covereth all.
Children have no need to be baptised. I really think this shows just how
merciful and amazing the atonement really is! Moroni 8 teaches us a truth
about the gospel and about Jesus Christ that could be atained no other way.
Such a cool chapter.
Anyway the thought has less to do with that idea, although I think its VERY
neat.
16 Wo be unto them that shall pervert the ways of the Lord after this
manner, for they shall perish except they repent. Behold, I speak with
boldness, having authority from God; and I fear not what man can do; for
perfect love casteth out all fear.
What a powerful verse for any who seek to teach. Speak with boldness, you
have the authority of God. Also, perfect love casteth out fear. Fear not
man, fear God. Pretty dope verse. I uh, am a teeny bit short on time so
thats all you're getting anaylasis-wise on the verse haha! Jesus is dope
y'all!
I hope you liked my thought :-)
Anyway, other cool stuff that happened this week!
We had Christmas conference this week in Beaumont, so we had to drive an
hour and a half. It was pretty awesome though, we listened to some pretty
dope talks and testimonies and then we got do to a talent show. All of my
district did a Mexican tap dance, so if you wanna see that check out my
photo album: (https://photos.app.goo.gl/LvH1u7A88T9LSwoS8)
We also went thrifting beforehand to try to find some christmas sweaters
and stuff for the dance. The greatest find was a $14 fake lego set that
looked basically real. Pretty dope.
On Sunday, we got our friend named Fabiola to come to church! We've been
trying for the better part of 6 weeks so that was amazing. Hopefully she
can keep progressing like this. Its so cool to watch people progress, I'm
excited to go to another area and help people there!
Thats kinda all that came to mind this week and so...
