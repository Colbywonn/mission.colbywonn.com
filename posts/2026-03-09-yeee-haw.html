Anyway let's get started with a little scripture action:

Ecclesiastes 3
1 To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the
heaven:
2 A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to
pluck up that which is planted;
3 A time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to break down, and a time
to build up;
4 A time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to
dance;
5 A time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones together; a
time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing;
6 A time to get, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast
away;
7 A time to rend, and a time to sew; a time to keep silence, and a time
to speak;
8 A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.

This is like so neat. Things are sometimes apropriate, and at other times
they are not. One of the grand secrets of life is figuring out when those
moments are. The other one is figuring out how to wait for them. Theres a
really awesome talk that I love from then Elder Eyering about this verse
and this idea. It's called "The Law of Increasing Returns" To sum it up, it
talks about how in life we are trained to follow the law of decreasing
returns. The gospel teaches us to follow the law of increasing returns. As
he puts it, some spiritual crops might take whole lifetimes to mature. But
they will mature. There is a time to plant, and a time to tend to that
which is planted, and a time to reap. For those who accept Jesus Christ and
His Restored Gospel, they will reap richly.
Just... go listen to it or read it. If you email me, I can send you an MP3
for it. Its an old talk. Or just google it. Worth it.

part of it, and specially glad to be in this part of the Lord's vineyard.
