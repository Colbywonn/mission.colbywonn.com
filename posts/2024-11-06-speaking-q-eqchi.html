This is from D&C 62,
3. Nevertheless, ye are blessed, for the testimony which ye have borne is
recorded in heaven for the angels to look upon; and they rejoice over you,
and your sins are forgiven you.
I like this scripture quite a bit, because it helps us learn a little bit
more about how the atonement works. We know that we are saved, "after all
we can do" (2 Nephi 25:23), but what really defines "all"? Why is there
such a discrepancy between the idea of "faith" and "works"? When does the
atonement kick in, and how can I access its power? There's a pretty common
misconception that there is a difference between faith and works. This is
simply not the case. In order to have true, living faith in our Lord Jesus
Christ, you must choose for yourself. The greatest blessing we have been
given as children of god, besides the atoning sacrifice of Jesus Christ, is
our agency. We are "free to choose liberty and eternal life" or "captivity
and death" (2 Nephi 2:27). How do we make this choice? Is it by simply
confessing our faith in Jesus Christ and living the rest of our lives as
the same, unchanged person? I would argue no, because we learn in Romans
that "every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God"
(Romans 14:11). I also love how James puts it, "Thou believest that there
is one God; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble" (James
2:19). From this we learn that something besides our belief in Christ saves
us. Everybody will believe Jesus is the Christ when he gets here. But faith
isn't just belief. Our faith can only increase when we act on it. Your
faith won't profit you anything if you don't actually do anything with it.
That brings me back to the D&C verse. When we bear our testimony, we are
acting on belief. When we act on belief, our faith grows stronger. When our
faith grows stronger, all the other things fall into place. It is through
this that our sins can be forgiven. What is praying for forgiveness but
bearing our testimony to Heavenly Father? If you had not faith, why would
you pray? Bear your testimony at all times, and in all places. That is how
you build lasting conversion to Jesus Christ. Remember and heed the warning
of Nephi: "from them that shall say, We have enough, from them shall be
taken away even that which they have." (2 Nephi 28:30). Keep bearing your
testimony. It will be recorded, remembered, and you will be made stronger
for it. You will never have a testimony too big to bear.

Elder Donnelly and I have been killing it here on Broadway street. We had

On sunday, while we were in the parking lot for church, we noticed that our

On Monday we had to go trade in our car from the Sunday incident, but
before that we went out finding. We were talking to a family that was
chilling outside, and we eventually learned that they were from Guatemala.
In Guatemala, they speak 24 dialects in addition to Spanish, although the
biggest is one called Q'eqchi'. I don't know much about it, but last summer
or something a missionary went viral in Guatemala for speaking it, from a
tv interview. His name was, and I have no idea how to spell this, massi
quatchlan. So yeah basically every Q'eqchi' speaker knows the missionaries
because of that, which is pretty funny! I know how to say basically "how
are you?" In Q'eqchi, and that was enough to get them laughing haha! From
there, we were able to give them the Q'eqchi translation of the Book of
Mormon that we keep handy in our car, and hopefully they'll start taking
lessons (They spoke Spanish and read Q'eqchi').
Yesterday was pday, and I got to go to the temple! It was awesome, as
always! We also were planning on going to a giant mall called the galleria
to ice skate, but it was closed :(. We just chilled in the mall for a bit
and then headed home and played Monopoly. Before the mall though, we got to
go to a Deseret Book, something which I probably can't do again while I'm
on the mission. I already have so many books, I have no idea how I'm gonna
get em all home! I bought a few books by Elder James E. Talmage, an apostle
from like 1915 or so. The coolest book I got, in my opinion, was Jesus the
Christ by Talmage, as leatherbound. Very cool!
So yeah all in all a pretty good week! Thanks for reading my email!
