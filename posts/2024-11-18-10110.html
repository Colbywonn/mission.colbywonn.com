Spiritual thought:
Today's scripture comes from Alma chapter 5. This chapter is kinda the
questions chapter haha. I'm not sure if any other chapter has as many
questions as this one. One of my favorite questions of the entire chapter
is verse 26:
And now behold, I say unto you, my brethren, if ye have experienced a
change of heart, and if ye have felt to sing the song of redeeming love, I
would ask, can ye feel so now?
In other words, if you have felt the holy ghost before, can you feel it
now? If not, you need to change whatever you're doing. Alma goes on to say,
Have ye walked, keeping yourselves blameless before God? Could ye say, if
ye were called to die at this time, within yourselves, that ye have been
sufficiently humble? That your garments have been cleansed and made white
through the blood of Christ, who will come to redeem his people from their
sins?
Behold, are ye stripped of pride? I say unto you, if ye are not ye are not
prepared to meet God. Behold ye must prepare quickly; for the kingdom of
heaven is soon at hand, and such an one hath not eternal life.
Holy smokes. That question is kinda crazy. If you were to die right now,
would you be ready to meet God? I don't think I'd be ready! I really like
the council of Alma to try to live each day as if we would meet God at the
end of it. I also think that it's interesting how Alma says "the kingdom of
God is at hand". There is a difference between the kingdom of God, and the
kingdom of Heaven. The kingdom of God is the church, while the kingdom of
heaven is the celestial kingdom. The church is also the kingdom of heaven
on Earth. So basically, Alma is saying that the authority and knowledge of
God is already with us, and so you need to repent and be ready NOW. It also
reminds me of what President Nelson said in his most recent General
Conference talk, "It is neither too early nor too late for you to become a
devout disciple of Jesus Christ." The time is now. This moment, right now,
is the only moment you get to choose. You can't change the past, and you
can only influence the future. Choose ye this day whom ye shall serve.
Choose the Lord. I know you will be blessed. The atonement is real. It
heals. Use it. Strive to be ready, during each moment of your life, to meet
God again. Begin singing the song of *redeeming* love again. Strive for the
spirit, and it will strive with you. Be redeemed. Please.
