Ok it's stuff time!:
D&C 123:
17 Therefore, dearly beloved brethren, let us cheerfully do all things that
lie in our power; and then may we stand still, with the utmost assurance,
to see the salvation of God, and for his arm to be revealed.
Good scripture! I really like this message, especially from where it comes
from. This section is the third of three that were written in Liberty Jail.
Uhh, Liberty Jail was not a great time for Joseph Smith haha. He was stuck
in there for something like 9 months with the majority of it being in the
dead of winter. Brother Joseph drscribed the jail as being like hell,
surrounded with demons. Not very cool haha. The jail was too tall for them
to stand up, they were fed awful, awful food and were poisoned on multiple
occasions.
In a time like thay, how do you think you would react? I think I would
probably tell my group of followers to free me, to solve this injustice, to
get justice!
No.
Joseph Smith demonstrated christlike charity in this moment. He asked for
help, yee, but for those who suffered for following him. He asked to plea
with the government for aid. He advised them to do everything they could,
cheerfully and happily. All we need to do is everything we can. God will do
the rest. Theres an awesome talk I have saved by Jeffrey R. Holland called
"Lessons From Liberty Jail" (
https://speeches.byu.edu/talks/jeffrey-r-holland/lessons-liberty-jail/),
that I would strongly recommend you to read or listen to if you have time.
Like a lot of President Holland's stuff, its baller! How blessed are we
that we have trials, how can we possibly grow without knowing how far we
can stretch?
Now, I'm not saying life in Orange has been like Liberty Jail, but I am
saying that its been a bit rough haha. I've been struggling quite a bit to
be diligent and keep working hard, but I know that diligence does bring
blessings so we're working harder at working hard haha.
Anyway other stuff this week!
