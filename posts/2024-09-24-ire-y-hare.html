LETTER 011  —  TUESDAY, 24 SEPTEMBER 2024 · week 14

Iré y haré

Buenas días amigos y familia,
How are y'all? I'm having fun down here in Houston! Yesterday was pretty
dang busy and so I'm sending my email out a day late so I hope I didn't
ruin the flow of your Monday. This week was pretty slow, as far as
missionary work goes but it was a great week nonetheless! Ok its time for
the weekly scripture!
Ok this comes from Isaiah 53 (or Mosiah 14, shoutout to my boy Abanadi):
4 Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows: yet we did
esteem him stricken, smitten of God, and afflicted.
5 But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our
iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his
stripes we are healed.
We've been focusing on the atonment as a mission this week, and this is one
of my favorite scriptures about the atonement. Jesus Christ has borne our
griefs, and because of his sacrifice we are healed. Sometimes, though, it
feels like it can be hard to access the atonment. Weather it be pride,
fear, guilt, know this: your sins are forgiven. All you need to do is ask
for it. Pray to our Heavenly Father in His name. I promise you'll be
forgiven. There isn't a single thing that won't be forgiven through Him.
Truly He is the savior of the world, but more importantly, he is your
savior. He knows your name. He took this burden upon himself for you.
President Schmutz gave a good analogy about this during our Sunday night
devotional: Jesus has already bought the food, it doesn't make sense to not
eat the food because you don't want to make him buy it. The price is paid,
the atonement infinite. Come unto him and "buy milk and honey, without
money and without price." (2 Nephi 26). There are lots and lots of amazing
scriptures about the healing power of the atonement, as well as general
conference talks.
Also General Conference in a few weeks, that'll be awesome!!!
Anyway, as for stuff that happened this week to me, I got to meet a bunch
of the people in my district and the English district that serve in
Broadway so that was awesome! We've been playing soccer and volleyball
which has been pretty fun. Our friends are slowly but surely progressing
which is also awesome! Nobody new on date yet for baptism, but I think
we're pretty close with a few people we're teaching which is dope.
Yesterday, we got to do our zone pday! So basically all of the missionaries
serving in Houston proper got together to hang out. We went to a park on
the beach called Sylvan Beach, and just played volleyball and stuff like
that. It was pretty dang fun, but I got a wee bit sunburned.
Something really interesting happened this morning, too. We got a call from
President Schmutz, and apparently an emergency transfer was initiated last
night (everything is ok) and basically one of the Elders in our district is
now a zone leader and Elder Ostler (my companion) is now the district
leader! I'm not sure why the switch happened but yeah kinda interesting.
That's kinda all I have to report for this week! I hope you enjoyed my
email and please enjoy your week! I love ya lots!
