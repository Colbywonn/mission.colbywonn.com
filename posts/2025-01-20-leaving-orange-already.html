Spiritual thought:
So in the church we sometimes really like speculating on certain things we
dont fully understand. What does heaven look like? What age am I gonna be
when I'm resurrected? Stuff like that. There are lots and lots of theories
about all sorts of gospel things. The scriptures tell us that if we ask we
shall recive. That can be a bit dangerous, though. Spiritual knowledge is
very, VERY powerful.
Here's a verse from Jacob chapter 4:
14 But behold, the Jews were a stiffnecked people; and they despised the
words of plainness, and killed the prophets, and sought for things that
they could not understand. Wherefore, because of their blindness, which
blindness came by looking beyond the mark, they must needs fall; for God
hath taken away his plainness from them, and delivered unto them many
things which they cannot understand, because they desired it. And because
they desired it God hath done it, that they may stumble.
Their fall came from looking beyond the mark. We are in the same exact
spiritual peril right now. When we focus on things beyond that which is
needed, we risk stumbling and losing sight of God and our eternal purpose.
So, remember. God will give you what you ask Him for. Will it be good for
you?
This isnt an invitation to stop studying and stop learning. This is an
invitation to always, ALWAYS prioritize the basics. There is nothing more
nor nothing less to our salvation than the gospel of Jesus Christ. If you
don't have a firm understanding of what that is, please study 2 Nephi 31
and 3 Nephi 11. I promise you that concreting your understanding in the
gospel, the good news, the rock, will help you a lot with your testimony.
There's no need to get lost.
As Jesus the Christ says in 3 Nephi 11:
39 Verily, verily, I say unto you, that this is my doctrine, and whoso
buildeth upon this buildeth upon my rock, and the gates of hell shall not
prevail against them.
40 And whoso shall declare more or less than this, and establish it for my
doctrine, the same cometh of evil, and is not built upon my rock; but he
buildeth upon a sandy foundation, and the gates of hell stand open to
receive such when the floods come and the winds beat upon them.
Also, the only unforgivable sins are ones predicated on knowledge. For
example, to deny the hoky ghost after having known him. That doesn't really
mean personal apostasy after being a baptised and confirmed member. We
operate off of faith, we are held a lot more lenient than say, the Brother
of Jared. That guy SAW God. Like, he knew, without a doubt that god was
real because he saw and spoke to him. Thats kinda why he got yelled at so
much for being disobedient. Knowledge is power, and with great power comes
great responsibility.
Kinda random string of thoughs but I hope you liked.

Anyway yeah stuff that happened this week! We got transfers this morning,

Not entirely nothing though! We have one really cool guy we're teaching
