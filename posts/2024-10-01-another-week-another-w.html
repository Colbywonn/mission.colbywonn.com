Another Week, Another W

Yo yo yo whats hangin my homies at home,
I hooe you've all had a splendid week and are currently enjoying your
Tuesday. My week was pretty... normal. Wow. I did get to go to the temple
today so that was hecking lit, anyway here's my spiritual thought!
Ok its 2 parts:
Helaman 5:
30. And it came to pass when they heard this voice, and beheld that it was
not a voice of thunder, neither was it a voice of a great tumultuous noise,
but behold, it was a still voice of perfect mildness, as if it had been a
whisper, and it did pierce even to the very soul
And the other part:
1 Kings 19:
11. And he said, Go forth, and stand upon the mount before the Lord. And,
behold, the Lord passed by, and a great and strong wind rent the mountains,
and brake in pieces the rocks before the Lord; but the Lord was not in the
wind: and after the wind an earthquake; but the Lord was not in the
earthquake:
12. And after the earthquake a fire; but the Lord was not in the fire: and
after the fire a still small voice.
I wanted to focus my scriptures on feeling the spirit this week, because
general conference is this week!!! We are very blessed to have a prophet
and amazing leadership in our church we can listen to every general
conference. It's important to spend time preparing for general conference.
I didn't really prepare for the last one and I kinda regret it :/. There
was a lot of good stuff in April and there's gonna be good stuff this
conference, too. You might be asking, "Elder Miller, how do I prepare for
general conference?" And well, thats a pretty good question. Let's ask the
scriptures. His voice is in a still small voice. How can we use this to
learn how we need to prepare? Well, much like a hispanic on a phone call,
you need to talk first for Him to respond. Seek prayerfully for questions
this next week, and see if they are not answered. You need to listen. His
voice will "pierce you to the very soul". So my challenge to all of y'all
is to get together some questions for confrence and then seek answers for
them through His prophets. We've got a lot of distractions in our lives,
(earthquakes, fire, thunder, &c.), but I know that if you take the time to
listen to the spirit you will be blessed!
As for the rest of my week, it was pretty fantastic! Due to an emergency
transfer, Elder Ostler became our new district leader (I don't remember if
I told y'all last week, think I did)! But he also had to go to District
Leader Training (DLC) so I got to do a blitz with an English elder named
Elder Finch. It was dope, we went out and found some english peeps for him
and some spanish peeps for me. I also did my first contact entirely by
myself in Spanish which was dope! I think I'm finally starting to
understand and effectively respond to more Spanish which is great.
Our people we have on date are progressing which is also awesome! One of
the people we have on date is from Columbia, and so they drink a lotta
coffee there. We taught the word of wisdom to her and she seemed to
understand it pretty good so that's awesome! The issue is getting our
friends to church though so please pray for us so we can get some of our
friends to watch general conference with us 🙏
We have a few other friends that are progressing too, we're hoping to get
another 2 people on date for baptism this week. With God, all things are
possible!
Today, I bought a magic deck to play with some of the Elders in my
district, and I'm very hyped. The closest store I could find with cheapish
decks was all the way by the temple, because the area by us doesn't really
have any. I guess Magic isn't very popular in Latin America haha.
But yeah now I'm headed to the church to get a much-needed haircut and to
try out my new magic deck! That's all I got this week so I'll talk to y'all
on Monday!
