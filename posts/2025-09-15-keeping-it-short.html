LETTER 028  —  MONDAY, 15 SEPTEMBER 2025 · week 65

Keeping it short

Good evening guys,
I hope everything is really well with you! This week was pretty long, but
pretty good. Lots of stuff going on. Lots of work! Lots of trying to find
people, not a whole lot of people progressing, but here we are still
trying! Just want to let you all know that I really appreciate your prayers
and also your time for reading this. All right here's the scripture:
1 Corinthians 13
1 Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not
charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal.
To quote Huey Luis, "That's the power of love!" Love really is what makes
him breaks every single part of our lives. We can do things without love,
but you really can feel the difference between doing something with love
and doing it out of obligation. You can feel it in missionary work, you can
feel it a normal work, and you can feel it in school work, haha. You could
share the truest thing on earth, and it wouldn't mean a thing without
charity, or without love. One of my favorite parts of being a missionary is
being able to share things by the power of the Holy ghost, because the Holy
Ghost Beyond just bringing powerful feelings of peace is a manifestation of
God's love for us. I am so grateful to be able to bring the power of the
Holy Ghost into people's lives and help them feel God's love (and my love
for them too haha).
Anyway, in terms of things that happened this week, lots of knocking doors,
lots of talking to gray dots ("talking", not a lot of doors were answered
haha), and contacting lots of members. We had some fun exchanges with some
people in our Zone, and got to learn some more stuff. Today for p-day, we
played pickleball, and then after ate some Costco pizza! That was awesome.
The week before, we also were able to go to Costco. Man, I missed that
place. Well, it's getting pretty late so I need to get this email sent out.
Not a whole lot of photos this week, so if you want to see them I guess
you'll just got to join my Google Photos. Good night!
https://photos.app.goo.gl/LvH1u7A88T9LSwoS8
With love and respect (and no tinkling)
Elder Miller
