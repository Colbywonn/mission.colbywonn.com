LETTER 019  —  MONDAY, 28 APRIL 2025 · week 44

20 days later...

Whoops. I forgot to do 2 weeks worth of emails. My bad.
Anyway, how's it going?
A LOT has happened in the past two weeks.
I got transferred (I didn't move rooms though), I baptised someone, we
baptised someone else this week, and I gave a talk in church on Sunday (the
first time (big deal))! Oh wow, long 2 weeks.
I got moved to the Jacinto City and Denver Harbor area, which is literally
in the same district as I was before. My companion is Elder Condie! This is
my 5th transfer being in the same district as him.
It was honestly the best news that I wasn't moving districts, because it
meant I was able to baptise Fermin! Elder Soucie and I found Fermin, and
Elder Sanchez were going to stop teaching him because he hadn't answered
the door in a while. On that time, the last knock. He answered the door! He
told us, "I got my work moved so I can come to church every Sunday!!" We
were like, "Dang bro, ok!" And then 3 weeks later, he got baptised!
Watching the change in his life has been amazing. He has already invited
his friends to come to church with him. In fact, one of his friends already
has a baptismal date! Such a powerful experience. I love Fermin.
We also baptised a 15 year old girl. Because she's under 18, I feel like I
shouldn't say her name? Idk. Anyway, her niece (who is 12) was baptised
like 2 weeks ago, and they are the only active 2 people in their household.
Such an amazing thing to see. We baptised her on Saturday, and got her a
temple recommend on Sunday. Boom.
We're teaching a few people right now who are close to baptism, but yeah
I'm still getting used to the new area.
Last week, we went to the temple! Very fun. We actually made it on time to
our endowment session, which was nice. We went to my favorite, cidercade
too! Yesterday, I had to give a talk in sacrement meeting. Scary. Having to
talk Spanish in front of everyone was something new for sure. I talked
about service.
Today was zone p-day. It was a wee bit of a fiasco, but it was fun-ish. We
kinda snuck off halfway through to play volleyball. Twas fun, too.
