Hey hey hey hey (hi!!!!!!)
Its me Elder Miller, I hope you didnt forget about me because I ran outta
time to write an email last week! Well, I'm here today to let you know what
I've been up to I suppose. Houston has been great, the work has been
smooth, and my spanish is getting better every day. But first, my scripture
of the week (tm pending):
If you only read one general conference talk this conference, make it
President Nelson's.
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2024/10/57nelson?lang=eng
I really love how much it focuses on:
A. The second coming of Christ.
B. The hastening of His work.
C. Our necessary, immediate personal conversion to Him.
The end is near!!! (depending on your definition of "end" and "near".) At
any rate, I love how President Nelson's message was so focused on Jesus the
Christ to return. I love the title of "Millennial Messiah", which I belived
was coined by Elder Bruce R. McConkie. I love how President Nelson said
It's not to early and it's not too late to start being a disciple of Jesus
Christ. The end is near!!!
The Hastening part is true, too! Not only have something like 300 temples
been built and announced at this point, but the mission work is hastening
too! In our mission, we have been finding and baptising in this year more
than any ever year before. As an example, something like a year ago, the
entire mission hit a 6-week high of 170 new people being taught. This week,
our zone alone hit 210 new people. Every single one of our statistics is
higher. Is this because we're working harder than any missionaries before
us? Probably not. The Lord is really with us! He's gonna get done what he
wants done before He gets here again. Truly the Lord is hastening his work.
Now is the time to become converted to Jesus Christ. It is not sufficient
to have a testimony. Your testimony won't be strong enough to withstand
what's ahead. You need to be converted, entirely. What does that mean?
Frankly, I'm still working on that too. I love President Nelson's
invitation, however, to study the atonement EVERY WEEK. The atonement is
the greatest gift we as children of God have ever been given. It is the
only thing that lets us change and return to our Heavenly Father again. The
grace of Jesus Christ is literally infinite. No, I don't think you
understand. It's I N F I N I T E. Like, it goes forever. It doesn't
arbritrarily exclude one mistake you made one time. It's not even just
until you repent the first time, or if think you can do it from here. It's
there for every mess up, every imperfection, everything. We just need to
accept it. The price has been paid, all we need to do is ask for it. Daily.
That, I think, is the foundation of discipleship of Jesus Christ. Accept
him as your savior, and BE SAVED. Do whatever you need to do to continue on
the covenant path. The lord will never leave us comfortless. (John 14:18).
Use the atonement to become a better deciple. We can always do more. Figure
it out lol.
I hope you liked my spiritual thought! Go ahead and read President Nelson's
message again too!
Well, for the rest of the my last two weeks, I've been been craaazy busy!!
I have zone conference and general conference last week, plus english
classes and more! Unfortunately, we had to move the baptismal date of both
of our friends on date :( I think the next transfer missionaries can
probably get it done but we probably won't be able to get them baptised
this transfer. We met some amazing new people these past weeks too. The
highlight contact was definitely Leomar. Leomar had been referred before,
but he got really sick with TB and was sent to the hospital for a while. He
just got out, and basically how we met him was he missed the turn to his
house and saw us getting out of our car right at that moment!! He stopped
and invited us over that night, and we had an amazing lesson! We were able
to go over 2 more times in the past weeks, and it's been awesome. We have 4
ish more people that I feel are suuper close to being ready to be put on
baptismal date which is awesome! Besides that, I've been playing magic
(yes, Grandma, my mission president knows about it and I only play on
pdays), soccer, and just GETTING THE LORD'S WORK DONE!!! I also got to hit
the gym for the first time in a while, which was fun! It's pretty rough
some days but theres nowhere i'd rather be right now. Keep praying for me
and my friends, and I'll keep praying for all of you! Make sure to pray for
forgiveness, at least once a day. I love y'all, keep killing it (and dont
kill me for missing an email)
