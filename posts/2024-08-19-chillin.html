LETTER 006  —  MONDAY, 19 AUGUST 2024 · week 9

Chillin

Good evening party people,
I hope you've all had the most excellent past week and that your Monday
went swimmingly. I was quite busy today so my email is a bit late, and also
very short. Mayhaps I may share with you a small scripture:
2 Nephi 25:26,
And we talk of Christ, we rejoice in Christ, we preach of Christ, we
prophesy of Christ, and we write according to our prophecies, that our
children may know to what source they may look for a remission of their
sins.
I love this scripture because it literally is the purpose of the Book of
Mormon. The Book of Mormon is for the spiritual nourishment of us and our
posterity, So that we may share the truthfulness of the gospel with them.
Without the Book of Mormon, we are lost and we needed to find our way and
our true joy in Christ. Rejoice in Him, as the scripture says. We are
saved! We can repent because of Him! Repent, do better each day. That is
the core message of the Book of Mormon.
Uhh I don't really know what to talk about for this week. We Had 3 baptisms
on Saturday, which was awesome!!! A lot of the week has just been normal
missionary work, which is super fun! I really love finding and teaching
people. I also have a split tomorrow, which I'm super excited for, where me
and one of the zone leaders will work together for a day in our area. It's
gonna be dope!! Today was a very sportsy day. In the morning, we played
soccer, we went bowling, and then a little bit more soccer, and a little
bit of basketball. Very fun! There are a lot more animals in Houston than I
expected, And I think it's really fun to take pictures of them and pet all
of the kitty cats and doggies. My Spanish is slowly but surely improving,
so that is also very good. I'm mega sleepy right now so I'm keeping this
week's email really short.
Other cool stuff definetly happened this week and if I work a bit better at
writing this during the week maybe I can share some more of the fun stuff
that happened. (I still havemt finished writing about the MTC in my journal)
Thanks so much for reading it and supporting me, love you all!
