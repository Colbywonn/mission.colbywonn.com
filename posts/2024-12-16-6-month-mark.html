LETTER 021  —  MONDAY, 16 DECEMBER 2024 · week 26

6 month mark

Wow, maybe my email week counter is off but I don't think it is. 26 weeks!
That's six months! Phew. Feels like its been 2 months haha.
Anyways, hi y'all! How are you doin? I sure do hope y'all have been well!
My first 4 days in Orange sure have been different! I'm not in Houston
anymore haha!
People actually talk with southern accents down here its crazy!
But yeah let's get into it, here's the thought of the week:
Today's message brought to you by King Benjamin. That guy really went
crazy, huh? If I'm gonna be real I never really liked the discourse of King
Benjamin, he kinda really likes using words, but it's definitely grown on
me a lot haha. His discourse is VERY articulate, and therefore is pretty
easy to find supporting scriptures or related ideas. I'm focusing on
chapter 5 today,
7 And now, because of the covenant which ye have made ye shall be called
the children of Christ, his sons, and his daughters; for behold, this day
he hath spiritually begotten you; for ye say that your hearts are changed
through faith on his name; therefore, ye are born of him and have become
his sons and his daughters.
8 And under this head ye are made free, and there is no other head whereby
ye can be made free. There is no other name given whereby salvation cometh;
therefore, I would that ye should take upon you the name of Christ, all you
that have entered into the covenant with God that ye should be obedient
unto the end of your lives.
Very interesting to me. Sometimes it gets a bit confusing with the
scriptures on whose title is who. Both Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ
have a calid claim to "Lord", "God", "Father", as well as basically any of
those titles. God the Father and God the Son are one in purpose, and so for
the most part the difference is arbitrary. If Heavenly Father is saying it,
you can sure guarantee that Jesus Christ is saying it, too. At any rate, I
love how this scripture talks about Jesus Christ being the father of our
covenant. We do everything in his name, up to and including taking his name
upon us. Just like we do when we're born! I just think it's neat.
Anyway, it's been a crazy week so yeah I'll share a bit with y'all!
So first off, I'm finally in a new area! I talked about it a bit in my last
area, but right now I'm at a place called Orange. This place is a wee bit
different than Houston for sure haha! Everything is a lot further apart!
Our church building is around 15 minutes away from we live, which isn't too
bad at all. Another big difference with this area and the last is the ward.
We're in an English ward now! There's no Spanish branch or anything so
anybody who shows up and speaks Spanish has to listen to Elder Card and I
stumble through people's talks. Its a bit rough at times. We'll definitely
get a lot of practice in with Spanish this transfer.
We've also gotten to do a lot of service this week, almost every day. The
Sherriff's office of Orange hosted an event called "Blue Santa" and we
helped out by setting it up and taking it down and handing out presents and
stuff. We also helped a family in the ward move a new house onto their
property! Definetly a little different than how they do it back in Utah
haha! They just rolled it off a truck, put cinderblocks under it, and
called it a day!
Elder Card and I have been getting along very well, we both like a lot of
the same things which is cool. He's a dope missionary for sure.
We also drove down to Lake Charles just to see what all the hype was about,
its a pretty neat little town, too (no, like, literally we had a thing
planned with the zone but it just fell through. Nothing happened!).
There's about 3 native spanish speaking members in this entire ward, so I'm
gonna try to make real good friends with them haha.
I can't really think of much else to write about right now but yeah thanks
for reading my email!
