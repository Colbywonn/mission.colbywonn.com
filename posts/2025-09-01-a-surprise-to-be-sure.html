LETTER 050  —  MONDAY, 1 SEPTEMBER 2025 · week 62

A Surprise to be Sure

Good afternoon all my adoring fans
Sorry for not writing last week, I hope you'll forgive me. It was temple
pday!
It was a very busy week, from transfers to temple to an unplanned baptism.
Let's waste no time. Let's talk about grace.
What is grace? Well, basically, it's anything God gives us for free that we
don't deserve. Theres a LONG list of things that fit that list. Our lives,
our families, air, the commandments. Basically every aspect of creation was
created and then gifted to us. The supreme, all-encompassing gift of grace
was, of course, the atoning sacrifice of Jesus Christ. We don't deserve
salvation. Nothing we will ever be able to do will merit it. Like King
Benjamin says is Mosiah 2:21:
I say unto you that if ye should serve him who has created you from the
beginning, and is preserving you from day to day, by lending you breath,
that ye may live and move and do according to your own will, and even
supporting you from one moment to another—I say, if ye should serve him
with all your whole souls yet ye would be unprofitable servants.
Its a really good thing that the Man who collects our debts already paid
for them! Now, a question. If the debt is already paid, why must we perform
works? Why must we have faith, repent, be baptised, receive the Gift of the
Holy Ghost and endure to the end? Well, to put it simply, those things do
NOT pay the demands of justice.
2 Nephi 10:24
Wherefore, my beloved brethren, reconcile yourselves to the will of God,
and not to the will of the devil and the flesh; and remember, after ye are
reconciled unto God, that it is only in and through the grace of God that
ye are saved.
2 Nephi 25:23
For we labor diligently to write, to persuade our children, and also our
brethren, to believe in Christ, and to be reconciled to God; for we know
that it is by grace that we are saved, after all we can do.
Interesting. What is all that we can do? It is allign our will with that of
God's. Be reconciled. Or in other words, repent. God wants us to make
covenants with Him. That's why he convinetly also made covenants the way to
salvation! God wants us to live him, to trust him, and to follow him.
Grace is real. You can feel it when you repent, and seek forgiveness. God
will give it to you!
Anyway a quick breakdown:
This week had a lot of fun stuff happened. One of the coolest things
happened on last monday. On sunday, we had learned that there was a woman
coming to church who said that she had been baptized, but was very
interested in being baptized. We got her address, and visited her. She
asked, when is the soonest I can be baptized? And, well, because she had
already been to church 2 weeks she could get baptized the following sunday!
We worked with her the entire week, and we baptized her on sunday! We
taught her everything, it'll teach everything again, but she was so happy!
I love being able to help people get closer to Jesus Christ through his
church! In terms of other stuff, my new companion Elder Lofgran is very
awesome! He actually went to Olympus, and so we are basically neighbors! He
sprinted for them and he's a very diligent guy.
My brain is foggy thats about all I remember having happen this week. See
you around I guess!
