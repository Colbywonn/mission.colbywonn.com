Spiritual thought time!:
This comes from Alma 62, the last of the war chapters (I think)
41 But behold, because of the exceedingly great length of the war between
the Nephites and the Lamanites many had become hardened, because of the
exceedingly great length of the war; and many were softened because of
their afflictions, insomuch that they did humble themselves before God,
even in the depth of humility.
This was talked about in some John Bytheway talk-thing a while ago, on the
war chapters. I love his insight on this verse especially. Some of the
people used the afflictions to justify their hardening hearts, while others
used it to justify their softening hearts. This is a pattern we see
EVERYWHERE. ALL THE TIME. I think of the example of Nephi, Laman, and
Lemuel. The same experience(s), two VERY different outcomes. So I guess my
question for you is, how do your reactions to things change the outcomes
for you? And a follow-up question, what did Jesus Christ do? To paraphrase
Elder David A. Bednar, he would turn out. When all others would turn in,
and focus on themselves, Jesus would turn out and help others. This is
humility. It's hard. Oh boy, it's hard. But yeah, basically also to quote a
guy named Kendrick Lamar, "sit down, be humble." Selflessness is humility.
Another random thing i've been thinking about is a quote from some random
