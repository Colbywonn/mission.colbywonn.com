Umm in other news, spiritual thought!
Don't you just love the scriptures?
This is in Alma 22
18 O God, Aaron hath told me that there is a God; and if there is a God,
and if thou art God, wilt thou make thyself known unto me, and I will give
away all my sins to know thee, and that I may be raised from the dead, and
be saved at the last day. And now when the king had said these words, he
was struck as if he were dead.
This is the prayer that the King of the Lamanites, the father of Lamoni. I
really love in his prayer when he says, "I will give away all my sins to
know thee"
Anyway I was thinking about this verse, because of a contact I had last
week. It was with a few drunk guys, and basically we asked them what they
would be willing to do to follow God. It kinda flew over their heads, but
it's definitely an interesting thing to think about. What sins are you the
most fond of? Would you give them away to know God? Another thing I find
interesting about this chapter is the fact that the King originally was
willing to give away his whole kingdom to know. That's not what God needs.
Everything is already his haha. The only thing we can really "give" to God
is our actions, our sins. Hagalo pues.
