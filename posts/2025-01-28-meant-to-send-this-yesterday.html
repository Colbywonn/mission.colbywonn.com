But yeah scripture and then other stuff:
I was thinking about these passages from 2 Nephi 28 about the Devil:
20 For behold, at that day shall he rage in the hearts of the children of
men, and stir them up to anger against that which is good.
21 And others will he pacify, and lull them away into carnal security, that
they will say: All is well in Zion; yea, Zion prospereth, all is well—and
thus the devil cheateth their souls, and leadeth them away carefully down
to hell.
22 And behold, others he flattereth away, and telleth them there is no
hell; and he saith unto them: I am no devil, for there is none—and thus he
whispereth in their ears, until he grasps them with his awful chains, from
whence there is no deliverance.
From this passage we learn that the devil will use 3 main tools to drag our
souls and minds from the covenant path, ire, inaction, and ignorance. When
we feel these feelings, we are driving out the Spirit. The Spirit cannot
dwell with anger because, as Jesus Christ says in 3 Nephi 11:29, For
verily, verily I say unto you, he that hath the spirit of contention is not
of me, but is of the devil, who is the father of contention, and he
stirreth up the hearts of men to contend with anger, one with another.
The Spirit cannot dwell with inaction, either. If we have no desire to
learn, we cannot learn and we cannot be taught. If we are not learning we
cannot feel the spirit. Light is intelligence, when we walk the covenant
path, we are in the light of Christ and are learning. inaction leads to
ignorance. I submit to you that unless you are learning something, and that
thing is true, you are not feeling the spirit. Truth must be manifest for
the spirit to dwell within us.
Satan's number 1 plan is to divide us from the touch of Heavenly Father by
seperating us from his messenger. When we suffer from these 3 things, we
risk losing valuable messages and guidance from Him. Humility, in my eyes,
is recognizing when we feel these 3 emotions. When we fail to recognize
these, we are suffering from pride. Pride is willful ignorance of our own
failings, and unwillingness to submit to God. We are entirely in the
Devil's grasp when we are filled with pride.
A good question to ask yourself is, how do I recognize pride in myself? Its
pretty dang easy to recognize it in others, but its much, much harder to
turn inward and recognize it in yourself. My suggestion to you is to look
for those three emotions in your day to day life. Ire, inaction, and
ignorance CANNOT exist alongside humility. Humility is a gift of the
spirit, and so if you ask for it, you will recive it. Humility is not an
easy attribute to have, and so it's a good thing we have a perfect example
of humility to look to.
