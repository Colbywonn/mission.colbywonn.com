Howdy folks! I hope your weeks went swimmingly, I can hardly belive its
Monday again! In case you were wondering, the subject line is Java. I
didn't have any other ideas for a subject line so thats what we've got.
Scripture time!
Today's scripture is brought to you by the entire epistle of James. Go read
it, it's fuego. I wanna focus on chapter 2 today in specefic though.
14 What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say he hath faith, and
have not works? can faith save him?
15 If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,
16 And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled;
notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body
what doth it profit?
17 Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.
18 Yea, a man may say, Thou hast faith, and I have works: shew me thy faith
without thy works, and I will shew thee my faith by my works.
19 *Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well: the devils also
believe, and tremble.*
20 *But wilt thou know, O vain man, that* *faith without works is dead?*
James spits straight bars in basically the entire epistle. I especially
love this chapter and this particular part of it. I especially love verse
19. James basically says: "yeah, yeah, good for you. You belive in God. So
does Satan. Do more than just belive!" James 2 is a call to action, for you
to act on your faith. I also love how James's call to action is charity,
the true love of Christ. I love the Relief Society motto (and also the
Moroni and Paul quote) "Charity never faileth." We can always do more to
serve. We as members of the church (or as members of society in general)
have a responsibility to be Christlike. In every situation, ask yourself,
what would Christ do? And then, do that thing. I challenge all of y'all to
try to find a way to serve someone else this week. Be more like Christ this
week. Email me your results.
Today is also Russel M. Nelson's 100th birthday! Thats pretty insane.
Shoutout to my prophet on his 1st centuryday!
Ok anyway I bet you wanna hear about my week. (I hope so, because why else
would you be here?) This week was pretty fire. Last Wednesday, we had
something called TLC, where we had to drive up North to where the mission
home is (bout an hour away) to practice doing roleplays. That was pretty
fun, and they also fed us raising canes so that was dope. Elder Ostler and
I also got to teach English class at the church on Tuesday and Thursday
which was a ton of fun. It's really fun to just vibe with the people that
show up, try to teach them a bit of English and then also try to learn a
little Spanish! We got to spend a lot of time with members this week. We
had a really dope member lesson with one of our friends named Felix. Felix
is so, so ready to be baptised but he needs to get some stuff figured out
before he can do it (get married). We did get someone else on date last
night though which is amazing! Her name is Carla, and she's about our age,
and speaks English and Spanish. We have a lot of friends progressing right
now which is super amazing to see. Thank you guys so much for your prayers,
they're working!!! It's kinda sad that transfers are coming up, so our
momentum might stop after next week. Its also crazy that i've already been
here in Houston for 5 weeks (there's 6 weeks to a transfer)! I think the
craziest thing thats happened this week was that I was bitten by a dog :(.
Not like a small dog either it was a huge german shepheard. It didn't break
my skin but it did tear a little hole in my pants. We were visiting a
friend who has like 6 pretty aggressive dogs, and when we were leaving one
of em decided it would be funny to nip me :(. You can see really clearly in
my leg where I got bit though haha.
One again, thank you so much for your prayers and for your support. I love
you guys so much, and I know that y'all have people you love. Spread some
of that love around for me. Talk to ya next week!
