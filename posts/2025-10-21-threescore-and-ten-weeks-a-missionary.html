Threescore and Ten Weeks a Missionary

Hey y'all!
It was temple pday this week so yeah I'm just chillin here at top golf.
Tradition lol.
Great week this week. Elder Ula is a bit sick so we're going kinda slow but
all in all chill. The area is kinda slow but the people we are teaching are
dope! Scripture time:
Alma 32 time!
Wow, one of the greatest chapters of scripture ever recorded. For real.
When I think about the truth claims of the Book of Mormon and I feel
overwhelmed, I just think about Alma 32. Who else but a living prophet of a
living God could translate something like this? Shoutout my boy Joseph
Smith for real.
18 Now I ask, is this faith? Behold, I say unto you, Nay; for if a man
knoweth a thing he hath no cause to believe, for he knoweth it.
Knowing isn't faith. Simple as. Almost 100% of people you know just don't
know. They have faith. Knowledge ain't cheap. Faith is free. It needs to be
tried though, and the more faith is tried the closer to knowledge it
becomes. They are independent though. It's one thing to intelectually
understand the saftey features of an aircraft and another thing entirely to
be in one during a storm. So yeah, faith!
But in terms of other things that happened this week, we got to do an
exchange with the ZLs and I got to go with Elder Blodgett. It was nice and
fun. He's a cool guy fr!
We also got to play some basketball, and we had Zone Conference! I almost
forgot haha
I don't really have any neat stories or anything from this week so yeah
