¿Comó estan todos ustedes?
Wow late email tonight, and I low key didnt work on it at all this week so
this one'll be a bit shorter than usual. Know that I still love you!
Spiritual thought:
On Sunday, the Bishop in our ward gave an amazing talk about commandments.
He basically said that commandments are the wrapping paper for the gifts
God gives us. He also said, mas o menos, "if you are missing any blessings
in your life, go read the scriptures and find out what commandments you
need to start keeping to recive them. Every single commandment we're given
has a blessing associated with it. The gift of the commandment of baptism
is the holy ghost. The gift of the law of tithing is so many blessings that
you literally cannot have enough room to recive them. The gift of the Word
of Wisdom is treasures of knowledge, even hidden treasures. If you lask any
of these, try finding what commandment you need!
Keeping the commandments in and within themselves are blessings.
Reflect (once again) on the words of King Benjamin in Mosiah 2:
41 And moreover, I would desire that ye should consider on the blessed and
happy state of those that keep the commandments of God. For behold, they
are blessed in all things, both temporal and spiritual; and if they hold
out faithful to the end they are received into heaven, that thereby they
may dwell with God in a state of never-ending happiness. O remember,
remember that these things are true; for the Lord God hath spoken it.
Wow.
Ok, now reflect on this proverb from my late great grandpa:
"If you think keeping the commandments is hard, try not keeping them!"
Ok and finally on this from Alma 41:10:
Behold, I say unto you, wickedness never was happiness.
Commandments are there to help us grow and to bless us. I love
commandments! What an amazing method God has developed to bless us, to set
us apart, and to protect us!
What an amazing gift!
Merry Christmas!
Ok and for the weekly breakdown!
Umm I can't remember a whole lot of what happened this week to be honest
except for a few standout happenings. The first, we got shot at which was
INSANE! We knocked on the door of a grey dot (aka, somebody the
missionaries have talked to before but are not currently taking lessons).
They spoke English, but they had a few trailers out back that they rented
that spoke Spanish. When we walked back there we heard a super loud POP. We
kinda stopped in our tracks and looked at each other a little confused, and
then 4 more went off like real quick. "Ah, thats a gun" we said. Spun on
our heels and made a speedwalked retreat from those trailers. I dont think
they were shooting *at* us, but the shots stopped when we turnt back. I
guess they didnt really wanna hear a message that night 😂. We'll not be
going back there again...
We also had some fun times teaching the English classes, found some very
cool new people, and are trying real hard to get better at translating
sacrement meeting.
On Friday, we went to Vidor for our district council. Afterwards, we
decided to go to a bbq place that is owned by some of my friends who live
there. Turns out, they closed permanently the next day so it was a pretty
amazing that we got to go eat there! It was very yummy, I ordered some ribs
and brisket.
Today, we had our zone pday! We played pickleball and did a white elephant
gift exchange. I got a pretty cool bird button up shirt so once I wash it
I'll model it haha! I gave away a cookbook and a jelly-bean uno that I
found at salvation army. That place is legit haha! We also got haircuts
today finally so we're looking fresh.
Umm not much else happened that I can think of that is of note so that's
all folks!
