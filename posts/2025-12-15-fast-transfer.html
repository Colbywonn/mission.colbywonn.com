LETTER 035  —  MONDAY, 15 DECEMBER 2025 · week 78

Fast transfer

Holy cow, sorry I forgot to send an email last week! Here's this week
though.
How are y'all doing? I'm doing fantastic! We got our transfer information
yesterday! We usually get it on Mondays so that was fun. I'll be headed to
Pasadena, which is a city located in the Houston metro area. When I opened
transfer board, it said I was going to be with a guy named Elder Romero,
but Prrsident sent us a message after and switched it up. I'll be with an
Elder named Elder Gonzalez! He's a pretty cool guy, he's from Argentina.
Talks like it too, haha. Jo me jamo type beat. I'll be moving areas on
Wednesday! Another fun quirk of that area is that we'll be car sharing.
This is the first car sharing area in this mission as far as I'm aware. So
that will be fun to deal with! Last transfer this area was walking only so
it's a bit better now haha! I'll be car sharing with Elder Card and Elder
Schiffman, two elders I've been with before.
In terms of other stuff that happened this week, we put our friend Abigail
on date last night! Her brother was baptised recently, and they came to
church yesterday. We went over for a lesson last night, taught them about
the blessings of baptism, especially from the temple. It was dope.
Here's my little spiritual thought. Sorry it's a wee bit shorter, the day
caught up with me and its now sort of late.
This is a section I liked from Leviticus. Kinda boring book overall but
this was cool:
Chapter 26 btw
3 If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;
4 Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her
increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.
5 And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage
shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your bread to the full,
and dwell in your land safely.
6 And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none
shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither
shall the sword go through your land.
7 And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by
the sword.
8 And five of you shall chase an hundred, and an hundred of you shall
put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the
sword.
9 For I will have respect unto you, and make you fruitful, and multiply
you, and establish my covenant with you.
10 And ye shall eat old store, and bring forth the old because of the
new.
11 And I will set my tabernacle among you: and my soul shall not abhor
you.
12 And I will walk among you, and will be your God, and ye shall be my
people.

Holy smokes, do you want all those blessings? I know I do! God is a god of
miracles and will literally cause rain to fall on the righteous! Keep the
commandments, and these (as well as many, many more) blessings will be
yours.
Next week, the email will be in diary style so it should have a bit more to
it. Love you though!
Thanks for reading
