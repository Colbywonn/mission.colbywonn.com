Scripture thought:
Alma 37:44
For behold, it is as easy to give heed to the word of Christ, which will
point to you a straight course to eternal bliss, as it was for our fathers
to give heed to this compass, which would point unto them a straight course
to the promised land.

Ok, this is pretty cool. The Liahona, or the compass, guided the family of
Lehi to the promised land. The scriptures will be just as effective.
Usually, compasses only function well if you know where you are, and where
you want to go, because it always points one direction. The Liahona pointed
to where the Lord wanted the family of Lehi to go. The scriptures are the
same way! Guides and the internet and things not of God will do a pretty
good job of showing us where North is, but they will never be able to guide
us. Only the words of Christ can do that. Only the words of Christ can take
us to the promised land.
