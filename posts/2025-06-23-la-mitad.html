Ok a brief scripture followed by a briefer goodbye:
This is from John 4. We talked about it a bit at zone conference.
35 Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold,
I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are
white already to harvest.
This, for me, is about procrastination. With wheat (allegedly, I'm an
engineer not a farmer), when it is prepared for harvest its a gold color.
When it's white, you need to harvest it now. Its already been ready to
harvest. And thats how missionary work is. Even if it seems like you aren't
reaping very much. Every sheaf gathered is good indeed. This applies to any
other task too! What things in your life are already to harvest? I invite
you to consider reaping.
Anyway besides reading Hugh Nibley, working, and sleeping, not much has
happened. I played a game this week called Villanous that I liked. Its like
a disney card game thing I dunno. We also ate some elotes (rosted corn with
mayo and cheese) and tried not to crash out.

Happy birthday (to my mission) (and whoever else has a birthday this week)
